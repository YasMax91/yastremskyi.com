## 4. Design

- **`src/data/cv.ts`** (new): the CV's own copy, English only — headline, summary, achievements,
  four roles (org descriptor, intro, highlights, stack), side projects, ten skill groups,
  availability line. Dates, role titles, org names, languages and the email come from `site.ts`.
  Every figure is interpolated from `site.ts` (`groundwork.agents/gates/tests/versions`,
  `stats.*`, `experience`), never typed, so `npm run facts` keeps the PDF honest too.
  Emphasis is written `**like this**` and rendered as `<strong>`; no `set:html`.
- **`src/pages/cv.astro`**: renders from `cv.ts`. Screen keeps the site's design system (both
  themes). Print follows the file's structure in the site's fonts: masthead (name, accent headline,
  one contact line) · accent spaced section labels over a rule · achievements with accent bullets ·
  roles as `Org · descriptor` with dates right-aligned, role title in accent, italic intro, en-dash
  highlights, a small `Stack:` line · side projects · skills (AI rows full width, the rest in two
  columns) · languages and availability on one line.
- **Print root background** white in `@media print`, so the `@page` margins are paper, not theme.
- **Provenance**: `site.ts` header and brief §2 record the October CV as a source, dated.
- **`scripts/check-html.mjs`** gains three audits over the built HTML (section 5).
- **`scripts/cv-pdf.mjs`** prints the page count beside the size.

Rejected: the file placed as `/cv.pdf` (two sources again; Max chose the rework); rewriting the
shared timeline keys (would unsign Ukrainian lines and take `/uk/` out of the index — Max chose A).

## 7. Blind spots considered

- Adoption figures are point-in-time; site.ts requires `meta.asOf` beside them → the CV's
  achievements line carries "as of October 2026" (a small departure from the file's wording).
- Site fonts set wider than the file's Arial → 1,714 words may not fit 2 pages. If not, report the
  overflow and propose cuts; never shrink type below the file's (~8 pt).
- NDA (AGENTS.md invariant 2) names clients, not vendors; payment providers are vendors and come from
  Max's own confirmed CV → kept.
- The stale comment in `cv.astro:17-22` ("the PDF is Max's own file") → corrected.
- OG card, JSON-LD: carry no phone and no CV figures → untouched.
