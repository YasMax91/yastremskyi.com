Phase 1 — three home-page concepts

Same copy in all three, so the comparison judges design rather than wording. Every word traces to §2 of the brief; nothing is invented and there is no placeholder text. The gate simulator is live in each — try the keyboard: Tab to it, then arrow keys.

Viewport Light / dark follows your OS setting — flip it there to check both.

A — Editorial

Who it appeals to: hiring managers and CTOs who read before they scroll; it signals maturity rather than novelty and would look current in five years. What it argues: that the person behind it has judgement, because restraint is harder than decoration. What it risks: in a stack of portfolios it is the least likely to be remembered, and its warmth can read as “consultant” rather than “systems engineer”.

B — Instrument

Who it appeals to: engineers and technical leads — it looks like a tool they already trust, which is exactly the audience arriving from the Groundwork repository. What it argues: that the AI-tooling story is native to this person, because the whole page is built the way his product is. What it risks: dark technical UI is the most crowded look in the field, and a non-technical recruiter may find the density unwelcoming.

C — Structural

Who it appeals to: anyone who opens forty portfolios in a week — this is the one they will still be able to describe afterwards. What it argues: confidence, through scale and hard edges rather than through adjectives. What it risks: it commits hard, and a conservative enterprise reader may read brutalism as a designer's site rather than a backend engineer's; it also has the least forgiving typography at small sizes.

What is real here and what is not.
  • Real: every word of copy, the Groundwork numbers, the four case-study “subtle parts”, the gate simulator (it works, keyboard included, and it ships zero JavaScript — radio inputs and CSS only, so it also works with scripting disabled).
  • Not final: the portrait is a reserved frame, not a stock photo — it swaps for the real file in one line. Fonts load from Google here for speed of review; the real build self-hosts a subset, as the brief requires.
  • Not built yet: these are home pages only. Routes, both locales, the content model, forms, SEO and OG images arrive in Phase 3.
