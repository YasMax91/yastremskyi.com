Full licence text: public/fonts/OFL-onest.txt and public/fonts/OFL-jetbrainsmono.txt.

  onest-variable.ttf      Onest, variable weight axis, from the Google Fonts
                          repository (google/fonts, ofl/onest). Instanced at 400
                          and 800 by scripts/subset-fonts.mjs rather than
                          committed twice.
  jetbrains-mono-400.ttf  JetBrains Mono Regular, from the JetBrains repository.

These are the upstream originals, complete with Cyrillic. The previous sources
were Latin-only cuts, which is why Ukrainian pages could not have been rendered
at all: verified by parsing their cmap — zero Cyrillic codepoints in any of them.

They are the source for both pipelines: the woff2 subsets the site serves, and
the static instances the Open Graph generator draws with. One source set rather
than two, because two sets diverge and only one of them gets updated.

They are committed rather than fetched so a build does not depend on a third
party being reachable.
